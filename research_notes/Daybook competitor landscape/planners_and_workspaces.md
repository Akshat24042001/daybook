# AI Daily Planners, Task Managers and All-in-One Workspaces vs. Daybook (personal-productivity half)

Research date: 2026-11-23. Method note: direct fetches of sunsama.com, usemotion.com and akiflow.com failed (hosts blocked in this research sandbox), so most pricing comes from web-search snippets of vendor pages plus third-party 2026 reviews (Morgen blog, usecarly, Lindy, PricingSaaS, Capterra, techpresso "AI Tool Review"). Several of these are competitor-owned blogs (Morgen, Ellie, ClickUp, Akiflow write comparison pages about rivals) and should be treated as secondary. All prices USD unless noted; verify on live vendor pages before publishing.

Daybook feature key used below: NLQ = natural-language quick add; REC = recurring tasks; ROLL = automatic rollover (+carry count); RIT = nightly plan-tomorrow ritual (+streak); TRI = triage of unfinished; EIS = Eisenhower board; VxE = Value x Effort board; GOAL = goals in hours/sessions with pace/"behind" flags; PROJ = projects; CAL = calendar/timeboxing; REV = daily review; STATS; AI = assistant that acts on data; VOICE; MSG = Telegram/WhatsApp bot; MCP = MCP server.

## 1. Which daily planners do planning rituals + rollover + timeboxing (and which still exist, at what price)?

### Takeaway
The "ritual + rollover + timebox" core of Daybook is the defining feature set of an established category led by Sunsama (~$17-25/mo), with Akiflow (~$19-34/mo), Motion ($29-49/mo, auto-scheduling rather than ritual), Morgen (~$15-30/mo) and Reclaim (free Lite, ~$10-12 paid) as direct alternatives; cheaper niche planners (Tweek, Routine, Ellie, Llama Life, Structured) cover pieces. Daybook's ritual/rollover/timeboxing is commodity; a planning *streak* and visible *carry counts* are the only slightly unusual twists, and both are trivially copyable.

### Cited Findings
**Sunsama (closest match to Daybook's ritual model; still active)**
- Guided daily planning ritual; evening daily planning exists as a mode, [Sunsama Help: Daily Planning](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/usage-guides/daily-planning); [Sunsama Help: Evening Daily Planning](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/evening-daily-planning)
- Unfinished tasks roll over automatically at midnight; Ritual settings let the user choose automatic rollover vs. a prompt to pick which tasks to move (i.e., a triage step), [Sunsama Help: Evening Daily Planning](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/evening-daily-planning); [Sunsama Help: Daily Planning](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/usage-guides/daily-planning)
- Daily shutdown ritual reviews completed work and carries forward unfinished work, [Sunsama Help: Legacy Daily Planning / shutdown](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/daily-shutdown)
- Weekly objectives + guided weekly review with a chart of time spent on objectives vs. other work; weekly objectives do NOT roll over (blank slate each week), [Sunsama changelog: Weekly Review 2.0](https://roadmap.sunsama.com/changelog/weekly-review-20); [Sunsama Help: Weekly Planning](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/weekly-planning); [Sunsama Help: Weekly review](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/weekly-review)
- Pricing conflict: pricing-page plan cards reportedly show Pro $17/mo billed yearly, $22/mo monthly, while Sunsama's FAQ says $25/mo ($20/mo yearly); no free plan, 14-day trial, [sunsama.com/pricing (via search snippet)](https://sunsama.com/pricing); [usecarly: Did Sunsama raise prices?](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/did-sunsama-raise-prices/); [Morgen: Sunsama pricing 2026](https://www.morgen.so/blog-posts/sunsama-pricing) (Morgen reports a rise from $20 to $25 monthly and $16 to $20 annual); older ClickUp figure of $16/$12 likely outdated, [ClickUp](https://clickup.com/learn/topic/productivity/tools/sunsama/)

**Motion (auto-scheduler; still active, moved upmarket to "AI Employees")**
- Individual Pro AI reported at $49/mo monthly or $29/mo annual; Business AI for individuals $69/mo or $39/mo annual; "AI Employees" tiers are team/usage-priced (e.g., Light $99/mo usage-based per Capterra, Starter $49/user/mo per SoftwareAdvice); 7-day trial, no free plan, [Lindy: Motion pricing](https://www.lindy.ai/blog/motion-app-pricing); [PricingSaaS: Motion](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/motion); [Capterra: Motion pricing](https://www.capterra.com/p/214264/Motion/pricing); [Morgen: Motion pricing 2026](https://morgen.so/blog-posts/motion-pricing). Sources conflict on tier names; official page not reachable.

**Akiflow (inbox consolidation + timeboxing + ritual; still active)**
- Pro $34/mo monthly or $19/mo yearly; "Believer" plan ~$14.90/mo billed every two years (visible only after trial start); optional $19/mo AI Meeting Assistant add-on; 7-day trial; 40% student discount on Pro Yearly added late Sept 2026 (PricingSaaS); Akiflow may apply purchasing-power pricing in some countries (relevant to India), [usecarly: Akiflow pricing 2026](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/akiflow-pricing/); [Morgen: Akiflow pricing 2026](https://www.morgen.so/blog-posts/akiflow-pricing); [PricingSaaS diff 2026W40](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/akiflow/diffs/2026W40)
- Aki AI assistant and a hosted MCP Connector are included in Pro, [usecarly: Akiflow review 2026](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/akiflow-review/)

**Morgen (calendar + task timeboxing + AI planner; still active)**
- Reportedly dropped its permanent free plan in 2026 (14-day trial); ~$15/mo billed annually or $30/mo monthly; one guide still lists a free 2-calendar plan (likely stale), [Reclaim: Morgen comparison](https://reclaim.ai/compare/morgen-calendar-alternative); [get-alfred: Morgen vs Reclaim](https://get-alfred.ai/blog/morgen-vs-reclaim); [aisotools: Morgen pricing](https://aisotools.com/pricing/morgen) (conflicting)

**Reclaim.ai (habit/task auto-scheduling on Google/Outlook calendar; still active)**
- Free-forever Lite plan; paid from $10/user/mo (annual), ~$12 monthly, [Reclaim compare page](https://reclaim.ai/compare/morgen-calendar-alternative); [notis.ai: Reclaim alternatives (Oct 2026)](https://www.notis.ai/blog/reclaim-ai-alternatives/)

**Amie (calendar + todos + AI meeting notes; still active per 2026 review)**
- Pricing unclear: one review says from $8/mo, another says free plan with unlimited calendars/todos and limited meeting-note credits, [agent-finder: Amie review 2026](https://agent-finder.co/reviews/amie)

**Cheaper/niche planners**
- Tweek (weekly paper-style planner): free tier; paid ~$4.17/mo annual (Capterra: from $4/user/mo), [makerstack: Tweek review 2026](https://makerstack.co/reviews/tweek-review/); [Capterra: Tweek](https://www.capterra.com/p/238465/Tweek/)
- Routine (tasks + notes + calendar + AI assistant): Free tier; Personal $60/yr, Professional $120/yr per Griply, [Griply: best daily planner apps 2026](https://griply.app/compare/best-daily-planner-apps); [Ellie: Routine review 2026](https://ellieplanner.com/comparisons/routine-app-review)

### Inferences
- Sunsama is the single closest analogue to Daybook's planning half: evening planning ritual + rollover + triage prompt + shutdown/review + weekly objectives + MCP. Daybook's differentiators against Sunsama would be: (a) price (Sunsama is $17-25/mo, a lot for Indian users), (b) hours/sessions targets with pace ("behind") flags (Sunsama objectives are weekly, non-rolling, without pace math as far as found), (c) Eisenhower/Value x Effort boards (not found in Sunsama), (d) Telegram as mobile interface.
- Prices across the category cluster at $15-35/mo for individuals; nobody in the Western set targets INR pricing except possibly Akiflow's PPP pricing.
- Motion has moved toward team "AI Employees" pricing; it is less of a direct competitor to a solo ritual-based planner than it was in 2023-24.

### Gaps
- Official pricing pages for Sunsama, Motion, Akiflow could not be fetched; figures are from snippets/secondary reviews and conflict.
- Whether any of these show a "carry count" (how many times a task was rolled) or a "planning streak" was not verified for any product. Absence not confirmed.
- Current status of Structured, Llama Life, Amie pricing was not verified from primary sources. No shutdown/pivot news found for Amie, Reclaim or Morgen in searches (not proof of absence).

## 2. Which task apps have natural-language quick add and an Eisenhower matrix?

### Takeaway
Natural-language quick add is fully commodity (Todoist is the reference implementation; TickTick, Any.do and most planners have it). Eisenhower matrix is built into TickTick (full matrix is Premium); it is not a native view in Todoist, Things, Sunsama or Motion per anything found. A Value x Effort board was not found as a native view in any mainstream personal planner.

### Cited Findings
- TickTick's Eisenhower Matrix is limited ("Basic") on free and "Unlimited" on Premium, [techpresso AI Tool Review: Is TickTick worth it (Nov 13, 2026)](https://academy.techpresso.co/reviews/is-ticktick-worth-it)
- TickTick Premium price: $49.99/yr per Nov 2026 review (up from $35.99, which many 2026 pages still quote); regional pricing varies, [techpresso](https://academy.techpresso.co/reviews/is-ticktick-worth-it); [Ellie: Todoist vs TickTick 2026](https://ellieplanner.com/comparisons/todoist-vs-ticktick); older $35.99 on [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/170641/TickTick/pricing/)
- Todoist plans: Beginner (free), Pro $7/mo or $60/yr, Business $10/user/mo or $8 yearly; Ramble (voice capture) limited to 10 sessions/mo on free (shared with Task Capture Assist), unlimited/fair-use on Pro and Business, [Todoist Help: plans pricing FAQ](https://www.todoist.com/help/account-and-billing/plans/todoist-plans-pricing-and-billing-faq-Vq2z0HWL6); [Todoist Help: plans pricing](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/todoist-plans-pricing)
- Todoist Pro monthly price reportedly rose ~40% (annual ~25%) in Dec 2025 (secondary source), [Morgen: Todoist pricing 2026](https://morgen.so/blog-posts/todoist-pricing)
- Any.do Premium $4.99/mo annual or $7.99 monthly; Family $8.33/mo annual; Teams $4.99/member/mo yearly, [techpresso: Is Any.do worth it](https://academy.techpresso.co/reviews/is-any-do-worth-it); [Any.do llms.txt](https://www.any.do/llms.txt)

### Inferences
- Daybook's quick-add syntax ("tomorrow at 11am ~15m #admin") is essentially Todoist's syntax with a duration token; it is table stakes, not a differentiator.
- Eisenhower boards are moderately common (TickTick, plus dedicated tools like Priority Matrix). Combining Eisenhower + Value x Effort + rollover + goals pacing in one personal app looks uncommon, but each piece individually is not rare.

### Gaps
- Priority Matrix (Appfluence) current pricing and status not checked in this pass.
- Things 3 lacks Eisenhower and has no AI/MCP from prior knowledge, but this was not re-verified in 2026 sources, treat as unverified.
- Whether Todoist natural-language parsing supports durations ("~15m" style) was not verified here.

## 3. Which have official MCP servers or AI agents that act on tasks?

### Takeaway
"MCP server so Claude can read/write tasks" is no longer rare: Todoist, Sunsama, Akiflow and (reportedly) TickTick all have official/hosted MCP servers in 2026, and Notion has an official hosted MCP. Motion appears to have only community MCP servers. Daybook's MCP is commodity among the leaders; its value is only in exposing Daybook-specific objects (goals pace, carry counts, CRM).

### Cited Findings
- Todoist: official MCP by Doist, package `@doist/todoist-mcp`, hosted endpoint `https://ai.todoist.net/mcp`, OAuth; official Claude Code plugin; (an older listing pointed to Doist/todoist-ai repo, repo location in flux), [Doist todoist-mcp README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doist/todoist-mcp/main/README.md); [Socket.dev: @doist/todoist-mcp](https://socket.dev/npm/package/@doist/todoist-mcp); [mcpservers.org listing](https://mcpservers.org/servers/Doist/todoist-mcp)
- Sunsama: official remote MCP at `https://api.sunsama.com/mcp`, usable as a custom connector on every Claude plan including Free, [Sunsama Help: MCP](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/integrations/mcp). User feedback says it can commit blocks but not plan them, can't list channels, lacks tracked-time exposure, [Sunsama roadmap: MCP Server](https://roadmap.sunsama.com/integrations/p/mcp-server)
- Akiflow: hosted MCP Connector included in Pro; Aki AI assistant (in-app beta since March 6, 2025; also WhatsApp, Siri, email), [usecarly: Akiflow review](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/akiflow-review/); [Akiflow changelog: Aki in-app beta](https://akiflow.canny.io/changelog/aki-in-app-your-personal-ai-executive-assistant-beta)
- TickTick: a third-party blog says TickTick hosts an official remote MCP at `https://mcp.ticktick.com` (Streamable HTTP, OAuth or API token, covers habits/focus/countdowns); NOT confirmed on a TickTick-owned page, [usecarly: TickTick MCP](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/ticktick-mcp/). Many community servers exist, [PyPI mcp-ticktick](https://pypi.org/project/mcp-ticktick/)
- Motion: no official MCP found; community servers (rf-d/motion-mcp explicitly "not an official Motion product"), [LobeHub: rf-d motion-mcp](https://lobehub.com/mcp/rf-d-motion-mcp); [mcpservers.org: h3ro-dev motion-mcp-server](https://mcpservers.org/en/servers/h3ro-dev/motion-mcp-server)
- Notion: Notion Agent and Custom Agents act on workspace data; Custom Agents moved to usage-based Notion credits from May 4, 2026; full Notion AI included on Business/Enterprise, trial usage on other plans, [Notion pricing](https://www.notion.com/en-US/pricing); [Notion AI pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing/ai); [Notion Help: Custom Agents](https://www.notion.com/help/category/custom-agents/all)
- ClickUp Brain ($9/user/mo add-on per Oct 19, 2026 capture; one Oct 29, 2026 review says Brain is now bundled into all plans) includes "@Brain Agent", [PricingSaaS: ClickUp](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/clickup/diffs/2026Q2); [eesel: ClickUp AI pricing](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/clickup-ai-pricing); [techpresso: ClickUp Brain](https://academy.techpresso.co/reviews/is-clickup-brain-worth-it)

### Inferences
- Notion's official hosted MCP is evidenced by the existence of a first-party Notion connector (the research environment itself exposes Notion MCP tools), but no Notion doc URL was retrieved in this pass.
- "AI assistant that takes actions on data" is now standard in Akiflow (Aki), Motion, Notion, ClickUp, Todoist (Ramble/Task Capture Assist). Daybook cannot sell on "AI that acts" alone.

### Gaps
- Linear's official MCP not checked (out of personal-planner scope; prior knowledge says it exists, unverified here).
- TickTick official MCP needs confirmation from a ticktick.com page.
- Morgen, Reclaim, Amie MCP status not checked.

## 4. Which have Telegram or WhatsApp bots for task capture/reminders?

### Takeaway
WhatsApp capture exists in mainstream products (Any.do Premium; Akiflow's Aki started on WhatsApp). No mainstream planner with an official first-party Telegram bot was found; Telegram task bots are a long tail of indie/self-hosted tools without planning depth. Daybook's Telegram-as-mobile-UI is the most differentiated piece of its planning stack, especially for an India-adjacent audience, but WhatsApp (dominant in India) is already covered by Any.do and Akiflow.

### Cited Findings
- Any.do: "WhatsApp Integration: Reminders and task creation via WhatsApp (Premium and above)", [Any.do llms.txt](https://www.any.do/llms.txt); Premium $4.99/mo annual, [techpresso](https://academy.techpresso.co/reviews/is-any-do-worth-it)
- Akiflow Aki available on WhatsApp (started there), in-app, Siri, email; automations run in-app only, not on WhatsApp, [usecarly: Akiflow pricing](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/akiflow-pricing/); [Akiflow changelog](https://akiflow.canny.io/changelog/aki-in-app-your-personal-ai-executive-assistant-beta)
- Telegram long tail: Remind Me Bot (NL reminders on Telegram/Discord/Slack/WhatsApp, voice-note transcription, recurring), [Capterra: Remind Me Bot](https://www.capterra.ca/software/1096486/Remind-Me-Bot); Nirodha (Telegram capture, AI classification, reminders with snooze/complete, web dashboard, daily summaries), [Product Hunt: Nirodha](https://www.producthunt.com/p/nirodha); AI Secretary Telegram bot, [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/ai-secretary-telegram-bot); 2026 comparison of 4 Telegram task bots notes some lack any task-list view, [DEV Community](https://dev.to/naderevo/4-telegram-bots-for-task-management-right-in-the-messenger-comparison-in-2026-498)
- DIY/open source: PAPAI (NL task management for Telegram/Mattermost/Discord via any OpenAI-compatible LLM), [GitHub wkich/papai](https://github.com/wkich/papai); n8n templates for Telegram + MCP task manager, [n8n workflow 3656](https://n8n.io/workflows/3656-ai-powered-telegram-task-manager-with-mcp-server/)

### Inferences
- Telegram bots exist but are thin (reminder streams, no planning ritual/rollover/goals). Daybook's bot is notable for being the front end of a full planner, not a standalone reminder bot.
- For Indian users, WhatsApp is the bigger channel; Any.do at $4.99/mo already offers WhatsApp capture/reminders. Daybook lacking WhatsApp is a gap, not just Telegram being a strength.

### Gaps
- Todoist and TickTick: no official Telegram/WhatsApp bot found (only third-party/Zapier-style integrations), not exhaustively verified.
- "Skeddy" was not found in searches; unverified whether it exists/is active.

## 5. All-in-one workspaces combining personal planning with CRM (Notion, ClickUp, Coda, Capacities, Akiflow)

### Takeaway
Notion and ClickUp can approximate Daybook's combined planner + CRM via templates plus built-in agents, at $7-20/user/mo plus AI add-ons, but neither provides opinionated planning mechanics (auto rollover with carry counts, nightly ritual, pace-tracked targets) out of the box, the user has to build it. Akiflow is a planner, not a CRM. This "personal planner + light CRM in one opinionated tool" combination is where Daybook is least commoditized.

### Cited Findings
- Notion: Free tier for individuals includes Notion Calendar (and Notion Mail per en-gb page); Plus $10/member/mo; Notion Agent full access on Business/Enterprise, trial elsewhere; Custom Agents billed by credits from May 4, 2026, [Notion pricing (US)](https://www.notion.com/en-US/pricing); [Notion pricing (en-gb)](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/pricing); [Notion AI pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing/ai)
- ClickUp: Unlimited $7/user/mo, Business $12, Brain AI $9, "Everything AI" $28 (Oct 19, 2026 capture); a conflicting Oct 29, 2026 review says Brain is now bundled and Business is $17/user/mo yearly, [PricingSaaS: ClickUp 2026Q2](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/clickup/diffs/2026Q2); [eesel: ClickUp pricing 2026](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/clickup-pricing); [ClickUp Help: New plans and pricing](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/42972527662359-New-plans-and-pricing-options)
- Lunatask described as all-in-one encrypted to-do, habit tracker, journaling, life-tracking and notes (no CRM), [search listing, see ZipDo/ClickUp roundups](https://zipdo.co/best/personal-goal-setting-software/)

### Inferences
- A Notion user can build a CRM database + tasks database + calendar, and with Notion's agent/MCP get "AI that acts on data". The missing pieces are automatic behaviours (rollover, carry counts, streaks, pace flags), which Notion doesn't do natively.
- ClickUp has goals, CRM templates and Brain, but is team-oriented and heavy for a solo user.

### Gaps
- Specific Notion/ClickUp CRM template URLs were not retrieved (search returned none).
- Coda and Capacities were not researched in this pass (tool-call budget). Their CRM/planner fit is unverified.

## 6. Goal tracking with pace (Sunsama objectives, Motion, Lunatask, Amazing Marvin)

### Takeaway
Explicit pace tracking ("am I behind on my target?") is rare in daily planners. Sunsama has weekly objectives with time-spent charts but no carry-over or pace math found; Amazing Marvin has goals with check-ins and progress views; the only explicit "pace line" found was in Strides (a habit/goal tracker, not a planner). Daybook's hours/sessions targets with "behind" flags is one of its rarer features, but only rare inside planners, not in goal trackers.

### Cited Findings
- Sunsama weekly objectives + review chart of time on objectives vs. other; objectives do not roll over, [Sunsama changelog: Weekly Review 2.0](https://roadmap.sunsama.com/changelog/weekly-review-20); [Sunsama Help: Weekly Planning](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/weekly-planning)
- Amazing Marvin: goals with review schedules, recurring check-ins and progress views; combines to-dos, calendar, goals, habits, time tracking; $8/mo (June 2026 price check), [ToolRadar: Amazing Marvin](https://toolradar.com/tools/amazing-marvin); [Saner.ai: Amazing Marvin review 2026](https://blog.saner.ai/amazing-marvin-review/)
- Strides has a "Pace line" that calculates whether progress is fast enough to finish on time, [ClickUp: goal tracking apps 2026](https://clickup.com/blog/goal-tracking-apps/)
- No source found showing pace tracking in Lunatask or Amazing Marvin, [search, ToolRadar compare](https://toolradar.com/compare/goals-vs-amazing-marvin)

### Inferences
- Daybook's "target in hours or sessions + pace + behind flag" tied to daily task completion is a genuine but narrow differentiator; Amazing Marvin (highly customizable, $8/mo) is the most likely place a power user already approximates it.

### Gaps
- Motion's goal features and Lunatask's goal features were not verified.
- Amazing Marvin's exact goal-progress mechanics (hours-based? pace?) unverified.
