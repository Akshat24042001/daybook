# Lead Discovery, Buyer-Intent Social Listening and Contact Enrichment: Competitors to Daybook Radar / Hunts / Research

Research date: 2026-11-23. Method caveat: in this session, vendor sites (e.g. gummysearch.com, octolens.com) could not be fetched directly because of network restrictions. All findings come from web search results, many of which are third-party comparison blogs written by competing vendors. Prices that only aggregators report are marked "(aggregator)". Where sources disagree, both figures are given. Treat every price as approximate until it is checked on the vendor page.

## 1. Reddit/social buyer-intent lead tools: which exist, what they do, what they cost

### Takeaway
This category is crowded and cheap ($0-$60/mo at entry). Many tools already do Reddit keyword/intent monitoring with AI relevance scoring and reply drafting, so that part of Daybook's Radar is commodity. None of the sources found shows a Reddit tool that identifies the business behind a post and finds verified direct contacts for it. That step (post -> company -> proven contacts -> ready message) is the part of Radar that looks uncommon. The category also carries platform risk: GummySearch, the category leader, closed over Reddit Data API licensing.

### Cited Findings
**GummySearch (shut down)**
- The official notice says GummySearch is closing because it could not reach a commercial license agreement for Reddit's Data API. New purchases, upgrades and renewals stopped on 30 Nov 2025. Full shutdown of services and deletion of user data is set for 1 Dec 2026 (per the search summary of the official notice). — [GummySearch: "GummySearch is now closed"](https://gummysearch.com/docs/gummysearch-is-now-closed-6533h)
- The founder ("Fed") announced the closure on 6 Nov 2025. — [Failory newsletter](https://newsletter.failory.com/p/when-reddit-pulls-over); [bigideasdb](https://bigideasdb.com/gummysearch-alternative)
- Conflict: some third-party posts say the service shut down on 30 Nov 2025 or "Dec 1". The official page separates the end of sales (30 Nov 2025) from full shutdown (1 Dec 2026). — [stacklead](https://www.stacklead.io/blog/gummysearch-alternative-2026); [buzzabout](https://buzzabout.ai/blog/gummysearch-alternatives)
- One report says that since 11 Nov 2025 every new Reddit API app needs Reddit's approval. This is a direct risk to Daybook's use of the free Reddit API. — [Failory](https://newsletter.failory.com/p/when-reddit-pulls-over) (via search summary; unverified against Reddit's own docs)

- Signal/buyer-intelligence platform that builds person-level buyer profiles from fragmented data. Zoom announced a definitive agreement to acquire it on 2 Jul 2026; terms not disclosed. - Pricing: Essential about $2,500/mo billed annually (most consistent figure). One review site says $999. — [moderninbound](https://moderninbound.com/blog/unify-vs-common-room); [spotsaas](https://www.spotsaas.com/compare/commonroom-vs-unify)

**Others seen in 2026 roundups (not verified individually):** RedShip, RedditGrow (draft-only from $19), Pulse ($29), Prems ($49), Snitchfeed, Bazzly. — [redship](https://redship.io/blog/best-reddit-marketing-tools); [bazzly](https://www.bazzly.ai/best-reddit-lead-generation-tools); [prems](https://prems.ai/blog/gummysearch-is-dead-best-alternative)
- One comparison says most tools (RedShip, Subreddit Signals, Syften, Brand24) surface threads and suggest replies but require manual posting. — [redship](https://redship.io/best-reddit-monitoring-tools)

### Inferences
- **Commodity in Radar:** Reddit/HN/X keyword monitoring, AI relevance or intent scoring (Leadverse has a 1-10 score), "why this is a lead" summaries, and reply/DM drafting. More than ten tools at $19-$60/mo do some or all of this. Redreach, Leadverse, Subreddit Signals and Syften are the closest on the social side.
- **Less common in Radar:** (a) scoring against the user's own offer catalog. Most tools score against keywords or a product description, so this is only a modest differentiator and the line is blurry. (b) Unifying Reddit with job boards (Adzuna) and open web search (Serper/Tavily) in one intent feed; none of the Reddit tools found mention job boards. (c) Resolving the poster to a business and finding direct, proven contacts. No Reddit tool found claims this. (d) The "opening angle" plus a ready message across channels.
- **Risk:** GummySearch's closure and the reported Nov 2025 Reddit API approval requirement mean Reddit-dependent features carry existential platform risk, especially on free API access. Daybook's multi-source design (Serper/Tavily/Adzuna) partly hedges this.

### Gaps
- Could not load the Syften, Octolens, Redreach, Leadverse, ReplyGuy or KWatch pricing pages directly (network blocked). All prices are from aggregators.
- Could not confirm whether any Reddit tool does "find the business behind the post and its contacts". No evidence found either way, beyond the absence of any mention.
- ReplyGuy's current pricing is unresolved (sources range from $9 to $49+).

## 2. AI SDR / signal-based prospecting: which find leads from intent signals and draft outreach?

### Takeaway
Clay, Unify, Common Room, Amplemarket, 11x, Artisan, Persana, Origami and Freckle all combine signals with enrichment and/or AI outreach. Nearly all are priced for funded B2B teams (roughly $100-$5,000+/mo), are built around firmographic/database signals rather than public social posts, and assume a US/EU SaaS buyer. Apollo and Clay's free tiers are the only realistic options for a solo Indian founder.

### Cited Findings
- **Clay:** Plans reworked in March 2026. Starter, Explorer and Pro were retired for new customers. Current self-serve tiers are Free (100 data credits + 500 actions/mo), Launch ($167/mo annual, $185 monthly) and Growth ($446/mo annual, $495 monthly); Enterprise is quoted. Seats are free. Overage credits cost 30% more. — [marketbetter](https://marketbetter.ai/blog/clay-pricing-breakdown-2026/); [miniloop](https://www.miniloop.ai/blog/clay-pricing); [aitoolsbakery](https://aitoolsbakery.com/blog/clay-pricing/) (aggregator, around Oct 2026)
- **Clay Signals:** includes Job Postings (detects new roles, "analyzes job descriptions for urgency indicators and geographic expansion"), New Hire (hires in the last 3 months) and Job Changes. — [Clay Job Postings signal](https://www.clay.com/providers-signal-catalogue/job-postings); [Clay University New Hire](https://university.clay.com/docs/new-hire-signal-overview); [Clay job changes changelog](https://www.clay.com/new-changelog/job-changes-signal); [Clay intent signals](https://clay.com/intent-signals)
- **Apollo.io:** Free plan allowance is disputed: about 900 credits/user/year in one source, 75 credits/month in another. Paid per seat: Basic $49 annual / $65 monthly, Professional $79/$99, Organization $119/$149 (3-seat minimum). A Jan 2026 Capterra review calls the credits system "misleading". — [cleanlist](https://www.cleanlist.ai/blog/2026-03-19-clay-vs-apollo); [Capterra Apollo pricing](https://www.capterra.com/p/158696/Apollo/pricing/)
- **Unify:** pricing is badly inconsistent across sources ("free for 3 seats, $20/seat Base", "Pro $60/seat", "$1,460-1,740/mo"). Likely repriced more than once. Seat- and signal-volume-based. — [cleanlist](https://www.cleanlist.ai/alternatives/unify); [factors.ai](https://www.factors.ai/blog/unify-alternatives)
- **Amplemarket:** entry around $600/mo; Startup plan on an annual term; Growth and Elite custom. The Duo copilot is bundled, not priced separately. — [factors.ai](https://www.factors.ai/blog/unify-alternatives); [gtmflowos](https://www.gtmflowos.com/unify-alternatives)
- **11x (Alice):** vendor pages conflict ($3,750/mo annual vs. "from $36,000/yr"). Third parties estimate $50-60k in year one with implementation. — [altitudebiz AI SDR pricing index](https://altitudebiz.dev/notes/ai-sdr-pricing-index); [formanorden](https://formanorden.com/blog/ai-sdr-pricing/)
- **Artisan (Ava):** no public price. Third-party estimates range from $280 to $5,000/mo, commonly about $1,500/mo or about $15k/yr. Email-first; LinkedIn in beta as of early 2026. — [moderninbound](https://moderninbound.com/blog/artisan-vs-11x); [imisofts](https://imisofts.com/blog/11x-vs-artisan/)
- **Persana AI:** Free (50 credits), Starter $68/mo annual ($85 monthly), Growth $151, Pro $400, Unlimited $600, Enterprise custom. A reviewer notes a merger with Rox creates roadmap uncertainty. — [syncgtm review](https://syncgtm.com/blog/persana-ai-review); [toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/persana-ai)
- **Freckle:** Clay-like AI research/enrichment tables. Free tier with 250 credits and unlimited seats/tables (checked 27 Oct 2026); paid about $99/mo. — [thesdrlist](https://thesdrlist.com/alternatives/freckle/)
- **Origami Agents:** AI prospecting agents that research the web for leads matching criteria. Pricing unclear: "not published as of June 2026, est. $500+", later "Starter $99/mo, 7,500 credits, 3 projects", or "from $49". Unverified. — [agent-finder review](https://agent-finder.co/reviews/origami); [heyreach](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/best-ai-agents)
- **Common Room:** see section 1 (Zoom acquisition, about $2,500/mo).

### Inferences
- Origami Agents and Clay (Claygent + Signals) are conceptually closest to "AI researches the open web to find companies matching a need and explains why". Common Room is closest to "signal -> person -> outreach", but it targets enterprises and is now part of Zoom.
- None of these tools appears to take a freelancer/agency offer catalog and match it to public posts expressing a need. They model ICP-fit accounts plus timing signals for repeatable SaaS sales. Daybook's "match a public need to my offers" framing fits service sellers better, but it is an inversion of this category, not something it lacks entirely.
- Scheduled "Hunts" are table stakes here: Clay Signals, Unify, Common Room and Octolens all run continuously.

### Gaps
- Could not verify which of these AI SDRs draft outreach from a social post specifically, as opposed to from firmographic/database triggers.
- Apollo's free-plan credit count and current AI email-writing features were not resolved.
- Unify's true 2026 price is unresolved.

## 3. Job-post-as-signal tools

### Takeaway
Using hiring as a buying signal is mature and cheap through APIs (TheirStack, PredictLeads) and built into Clay. Daybook's Adzuna-based job-board scanning is commodity as a data source. The value Daybook could add is interpreting a job post as "this company needs an AI service I sell".

### Cited Findings
- **TheirStack:** free tier of 200 API credits/mo. Paid from $49/mo for 1,500 credits (one source says about $59). 1 credit per job, 3 per company. Job-posting plus tech-stack data for BD lists. — [workinsiders](https://workinsiders.com/tools/theirstack/); [revenueflow](https://www.revenueflow.com/blog/best-theirstack-alternatives); [G2 Apollo vs TheirStack](https://www.g2.com/compare/apollo-io-vs-theirstack)
- **PredictLeads:** up to 100 free API calls/mo, then pay-as-you-go with a $40/mo minimum. A Job Openings call returns up to 1,000 records for 1 credit (billed per request). — [hirebase](https://www.hirebase.org/compare/best-job-data-apis); [jobspipe](https://jobspipe.dev/blog/best-jobs-api-2026)
- **Clay:** Job Postings and Hiring Surges signals; New Hire signal (cost disputed: "5 credits per result" vs. "about 0.2 credits per check"). — [Clay hiring surges](https://www.clay.com/providers-signal-type-subtype/hiring-surges); [HubSpot x Clay guide](https://www.hubspot.com/startups/tech-stacks/sales-csx/identify-buyer-intent-hiring-signals-hubspot-clay)
- **Apollo:** used to find hiring-manager contacts with job-posting filters; no separate intent-signal pricing found. — [G2 Apollo vs TheirStack](https://www.g2.com/compare/apollo-io-vs-theirstack)

### Inferences
- TheirStack's and PredictLeads' free tiers are a cheaper and broader replacement or supplement for Adzuna, if Daybook wants deeper hiring signals.

### Gaps
- Apollo's 2026 job-postings filter details and plan gating were not confirmed.

## 4. Enrichment with verification/proof: does anyone show the source per contact?

### Takeaway
Mainstream enrichment vendors (Apollo, Lusha, ZoomInfo, Kaspr, Snov) return database contacts with confidence or verification status, not page-level proof. Hunter is reported to show confidence scores and sources. Source-URL-per-contact is mostly found in small scraper tools (Apify actors) and Firecrawl's open-source Fire Enrich. A policy of "no guessed contacts, proof required for every phone and email" is rare as a product stance, but it is easy to replicate technically.

### Cited Findings
- **Hunter:** a 2026 review lists "confidence scores and sources" as strengths (no UI detail given). Pricing as of Aug 2026: Free $0 (50 credits), Starter $49/mo ($34 annual, 2,000 credits), Growth $149 ($104 annual, 10,000), Scale $299 ($209 annual, 25,000), Enterprise custom. — [thefutureofthings review](https://thefutureofthings.com/25419-hunter-io-review/); [dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/hunter-io-pricing); [Hunter pricing page](https://hunter.io/pricing)
  - From training knowledge (not re-verified this session): Hunter's Domain Search lists the public web pages where each email was found. Mark as unverified.
- **Snov.io:** free plan with 50 credits/mo. Starter $39/mo monthly ($29.25 annual, 1,000 credits); Pro from $99/mo. — [snov.io Hunter review](https://snov.io/blog/hunter-io-review/); [supered](https://www.supered.io/blog/hunter-io-alternatives/)
- **Lusha:** free-plan credits are disputed (5, 40 or 70/mo depending on source). Pro from $22.45/user/mo annual for 3,000 credits. — [skrapp](https://skrapp.io/blog/lusha-alternatives/); [enrich.so](https://www.enrich.so/blog/best-hunter-alternatives)
- **Kaspr:** free plan with about 5 phone numbers/mo and 15 work emails/mo; paid from about $49/mo. — [moderninbound](https://moderninbound.com/blog/best-hunter-io-alternatives); [emelia](https://emelia.io/software/hunter/alternatives)
- **Source-evidence enrichers:** Apify "Website Contact Enrichment" (maydit) keeps the source page and extraction method for every signal. Phones come only from tel links or structured data ("does not guess numbers"). Apify "Website Contact Enricher" (samstorm) records the page URL, method, observation time and nearby text per contact. — [Apify maydit](https://apify.com/maydit/website-contact-enrichment); [Apify samstorm](https://apify.com/samstorm/website-contact-enricher); [Apify angadseth](https://apify.com/angadseth/website-contact-enricher)
- **Firecrawl Fire Enrich (open source):** "every piece of data is linked back to the URL it was found on". — [GitHub firecrawl/fire-enrich](https://github.com/firecrawl/fire-enrich)
- **Tomba:** its enrichment API returns a `sources` array. — [Tomba enrichment](https://tomba.io/enrichment)

### Inferences
- Daybook's roughly 50-field research with mandatory proof resembles Fire Enrich plus Claygent-style research more than Apollo or Lusha. The no-guessing proof policy differs from the big databases, which sell pattern-guessed or "verified" emails without page provenance. It is not technically hard, since open-source tools already do it. The moat is trust and UX, not tech.
- Proof-only contacts will have much lower coverage than Apollo/Lusha databases, especially for personal phones. Expect "no contact found" often. This is a real trade-off to position honestly.

### Gaps
- Claygent's citation behavior was not verified.
- ZoomInfo pricing and provenance were not researched (enterprise, quote-only; out of budget for the target user).
- Hunter's per-email source display was not confirmed from 2026 docs.

## 5. Event discovery for sales trips: does any product plan a sales trip around events and local businesses?

### Takeaway
No product was found that combines "I'll be in city X on dates Y" with event discovery, local-business prospecting and suggested meetings. Field-sales route planners (Badger Maps, Map My Customers, Zoho RouteIQ) cover nearby prospects and routing. Event platforms (Luma, Meetup, Eventbrite) cover events. Nothing found joins them. Daybook's Trip mode looks genuinely rare (absence of evidence, not proof).

### Cited Findings
- **Badger Maps** surfaces new prospects near any location by keyword and adds them to a pipeline. It is a field-sales routing platform; its event advice is only a blog tip to "explore local neighborhoods and events". — [Badger Maps](https://www.badgermapping.com/); [Badger travel apps blog](https://www.badgermapping.com/blog/best-travel-apps-for-sales-reps/)
- **Map My Customers** shows potential prospects near a location on a map. — [Map My Customers (2023, older)](https://mapmycustomers.com/2023/01/10/strongthe-5-best-tools-to-plan-a-successful-sales-trip-strong/)
- **Zoho RouteIQ** shows nearby CRM leads, deals, contacts, accounts and CRM events (not public events). — [Zoho RouteIQ](https://www.zoho.com/routeiq/)
- **Pin Drop and Mapview** map and route CRM contacts. — [Pin Drop](https://www.pindrop.it/route-mapping); [Mapview App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mapview-sales-route-planner/id426226106)
- **TripIt** handles itineraries only; Yelp and Resy are general local discovery. — [HubSpot sales apps list](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/apps-every-sales-rep-needs-on-their-phone)

### Inferences
- Trip mode is the most differentiated Daybook feature in this scope. Route planners are US field-sales and CRM-centric, with no event layer and no AI intent.
- A determined user could approximate it with Luma/Meetup search plus Google Maps plus ChatGPT, so the moat is convenience.

### Gaps
- Luma, Meetup and Eventbrite were not researched for APIs or "events near dates" features (out of tool budget).
- Conference-networking apps (e.g. Brella, Grip) that suggest meetings within a single event were not researched.

## 6. Free-tier / budget options for an Indian solo founder

### Takeaway
A $0 stack exists: F5Bot + KWatch free + Apollo free + Hunter free + Snov free + TheirStack free + Clay or Freckle free. Each is capped and fragmented. Paid social-intent tools start around $19/mo (Redreach, KWatch). Everything signal-based beyond that (Clay Launch $167, Amplemarket around $600, Common Room around $2,500, 11x/Artisan $1.5k+) is out of range for the target user.

### Cited Findings
- Free tiers found: F5Bot (free), KWatch (2+2 keywords), Clay (100 credits + 500 actions), Apollo (disputed: 75/mo or 900/yr), Hunter (50 credits), Snov (50 credits), Lusha (5-70 credits, disputed), Kaspr (about 5 phones + 15 emails), Persana (50 credits), Freckle (250 credits), TheirStack (200 API credits), PredictLeads (100 calls). — sources as cited in sections 1-4 above.
- Cheapest paid social-intent tools: Redreach $19 (weekly) or $39 (daily); KWatch $19; Subreddit Signals $29; Syften $29.95. — [leadmore](https://leadmore.ai/blog/redreach-review); [snitchfeed](https://snitchfeed.com/blog/best-reddit-monitoring-tools-2026); [mediafa.st](https://www.mediafa.st/alternatives/subreddit-signals); [okara](https://okara.ai/blog/syften-alternatives)

### Inferences
- **Brutal summary of Radar vs the market:**
  - **Commodity:** Reddit/HN keyword monitoring; AI intent scoring; plain-language "why it's a lead"; reply/DM drafts; scheduled searches; job-post signals; database-style email lookup.
  - **Moderately differentiated:** one feed across Reddit, job boards and open web search, scored against the user's own service offers (most tools score against keywords or an ICP); an India-friendly $0 cost base.
  - **Genuinely rare (no competitor found):** (1) post -> identify the business -> direct contacts with proof, in one flow; (2) a proof-required, no-guess contact policy as a product promise (only scrapers and Fire Enrich do provenance); (3) Trip mode (events + local businesses + meeting suggestions around travel dates).
- **Closest matches by feature:**
  - Radar social side: Leadverse, Redreach, Subreddit Signals, Octolens, Syften.
  - Radar plus Research: Clay (Signals + Claygent + waterfall) and Origami Agents.
  - Proof-based research: Firecrawl Fire Enrich and Apify source-evidence enrichers.
  - Trip: none (Badger Maps is the nearest partial).
- **Platform-risk lesson:** GummySearch died on Reddit API licensing. A free-tier Reddit dependency is fragile at scale.

### Gaps
- No India-specific pricing (INR or PPP discounts) was found for any tool.
- Brand24, Syften and Octolens free tiers were confirmed only as trials, not free plans.
